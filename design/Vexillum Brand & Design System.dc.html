The mark
A vexillum reduced to its geometry: staff, crossbar, and hanging cloth with a fringed edge. No banner curl, no ornament — a signal, not an illustration.
32px — header mark
16px — favicon
Single-color only — render as currentColor, never multi-tone or gradient-filled. Keep the crossbar wider than the cloth so both ends stay visible; that silhouette is what carries the mark at 16px. Minimum clear space: half the mark's height on every side.
Palette
Terminal-dark by default. One lapis accent — from the mármol & lapislázuli family shared with forum — used sparingly for the mark, links, and state, not for large fills.

DISPATCHING
No new interface to learn.
You already talk to your commander in Claude Code. Ask it to dispatch a soldier, and it calls vexillum for you.
> dispatch a soldier to refactor the payment module
Bash(vx dispatch "refactor the payment module" --kind mission --model sonnet)
↳ dispatched · camp/refactor-payment-module
↳ soldier running · sentinel watching
> also have someone trace that memory leak, just investigate for now
Bash(vx dispatch "trace the memory leak" --kind scout)
↳ dispatched · camp/trace-memory-leak
